- V3 seed records contain prize amounts/category labels rather than normalized tier/weight fields.

- V3 seed contains 39 draw dates before later browser-added draws; 2026 artifact removal leaves 38 draw dates in the bundled baseline because 2026-09-13 is represented by a web supplement and 2026 artifact cleanup does not invent missing draws.
